# Foster Insights Interview App

## Design

I designed this web app from the view of the DCFS staff who need access to data insights to assist them in their decision-making
and project planning. I used a Miro board to detail the requirements and give thought to what kinds of pages, data, and insights
the customer would want to see.

Chosen focus: Foster Home Retention
- Insights: I wanted to focus on providers who may not renew their license or have not been participating in the system. This could give clients the chance to evaluate the time and cost of attempting to involve these providers, as well as a jumping off point for who to focus on
- UI/UX: I believe a nice data dashboard with simple information is a great landing page. It gives the clients options and can easily lead them further into the data for whatever their needs may be. It's important for the app to be useful and engaging at the same time.

## Implementation Progress

Features Implemented:
- Complex data tables displaying the CSV data in app for easy reference
- A dashboard with insight cards giving the customer different possibilities for focus
- Several cards are fully implemented with functions and calculations, including the top 4 cards and the license risk circle chart

Features Not Implemented:
- The last 4 cards/charts were not implemented due to time. I did label the card so my intention is clear as far as what I might consider to be useful for the customer
- Expanding the cards to drill down further into the data. Ideally each card would open a page or model where the data backing the card can be browsed

## Technology Used

- Node.js / React / Typescript
- Hosted via Github Pages
- Material UI for all UI/UX components and styling
